Nexus Retail Group

Nexus Retail Group, a Canadian consumer electronics retailer, commissioned this SQL-based analytics project to address volatile sales and inventory margin erosion during high-inflation periods. The objective was to analyze historical transaction, inventory, and macroeconomic data to optimize stock allocation, improve promotional ROI, and build resilience against economic shifts. Key findings: Laptop sales fell ~10.4% during high-inflation periods, while Electronics grew ~22.3%, showing counter-cyclical resilience. Promotions delivered almost no measurable lift (725 vs. 775 transactions, active vs. inactive) and were poorly timed to demand (48.4% vs. 48.3% share in high vs. low season). Deadstock capital peaked in 2020 at over $1.01M, roughly 55% higher than 2022's low. About 19% of SKUs (264 of 1,375) sat as high-risk, single-sale stock. Electronics alone tied up $1.68M in above-average-cost deadstock, the highest of any category. The project delivered four actionable recommendations: inflation-aware buying plans, re-timed promotions, a slow-mover audit, and prioritized capital controls for Electronics..

OmniStep GLOBAL FOOTWEAR

This Power BI project developed an interactive dashboard to consolidate sales and customer data for OmniStep Global Footwear, a multinational B2C footwear retailer. The objective was to improve visibility into revenue, product performance, customer demographics, payment methods, and regional trends to support faster, data-driven decisions. Key achievements: Net revenue reached $9.1M across 30K orders and 75K units sold (4.0 avg. rating, 13.3% avg. discount). Full-price products generated $1.72M vs. $1.23M at 30% discount, with units sold barely moving (12.2K–12.8K), proving deep discounts erode margin without lifting demand. Revenue was evenly spread across categories ($1.77M–$1.85M each) and genders (Women $3.08M, Men $3.02M, Unisex $2.97M). UAE led country revenue at $1.54M, and Bank Transfer led payment methods at 25.62% share. Recommendations included capping discounts above 15%, aligning inventory with regional demand, and strengthening Q1–Q2 promotions to offset seasonal dips.

SALIENT RETAIL STORE

Salient Venture, an e-commerce retailer of household and lifestyle products, undertook this Excel-based analytics project to unify fragmented sales, customer, and product data into a single dashboard. The objective was to give management real-time visibility into weekly revenue, profit trends, top-performing products/regions, and customer behavior to support strategic decision-making and reduce reliance on a few high-performing products. Key achievements: Q4 2025 delivered $114.74M in revenue, $97.53M in expenses, and $17.21M in profit at a 15% margin, marking a strong financial turnaround. Texas led regional profitability, capturing 35.4% ($6.10M) of total profit. Gold Bracelets topped product demand (17.2% share, $783K), while Iron, Turtleneck, and Badminton Racket together contributed over 49% of demand. The top 10 products generated roughly $7.1M in profit. Customer concentration was high, with four accounts driving 85% of revenue. Youth and Early Youth segments drove 65% of sales ($37.1M and $35.1M respectively), and female customers contributed 58% of revenue ($9.25M) versus 42% from males. Recommendations focused on protecting core accounts, diversifying age and gender segments, and replicating Texas's operational success in underperforming cities..
